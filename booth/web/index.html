Check

Checks never change your videos. During a quick check the SSD goes offline for a minute; anything recorded meanwhile goes to the microSD and is copied over afterwards.

Quick check (1–3 min): connection, power, the system's error messages, the filesystem, and a write/read test. Full scan (slow): reads every part of the drive (read-only; pauses while someone records). Health report: the drive's own SMART report, if its USB adapter passes it through.

Repair & reformat

Repair the filesystem Fixes filesystem errors a check found. Videos stay where they are.
Reformat the SSD Sets the SSD up the way the booth wants it: one exFAT partition called CONFESSIONS (works on Mac, Windows and the Pi). Every video on the SSD is first copied to the microSD and checked; if there isn't room it stops without touching anything. The booth copies the videos back onto the SSD afterwards.

Software update

Version…

The booth uses your phone's hotspot to download updates. Its own Wi-Fi switches off for a few minutes, then comes back by itself. Videos are never touched, and it only updates when nobody is recording.

  1. On your phone turn on Personal Hotspot (iPhone: Settings → Personal Hotspot → Allow Others to Join, and turn on Maximise Compatibility). Stay on that screen.
  2. Tap Update now below. Your phone drops off the booth Wi-Fi.
  3. After 2–5 minutes the booth Wi-Fi is back: rejoin it and open this page to see the result.
